GestureKing adds freely configurable gesture areas to the edges of the Android screen.

Actions can be assigned to different areas and swipe directions. Available actions include system controls, media controls, and launching selected apps.

The position, size, and behavior of gesture areas can be adjusted. A visual preview helps with configuration.

GestureKing uses Jetpack Compose for its user interface. Shizuku is required for certain advanced actions.

Features:
• configurable gesture areas along the screen edges
• separate actions for different swipe directions
• launch selected apps
• system and media actions
• adjustable gesture-area size and position
• visual preview of active areas
• light and dark appearance
